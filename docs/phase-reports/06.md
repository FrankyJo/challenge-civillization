## Independent review

A `reviewer` subagent (Sonnet) found **0 critical and 0 high** findings. It confirmed:
- `assertFence`'s `FOR SHARE` serializes with takeovers;
- the lock order (world state, then lease row) cannot deadlock;
- tokens are monotonic;
- the schedule math is correct.

It found 5 medium and 4 low findings. All were addressed:

| # | Severity | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Medium | A crash between a pause or resume and its season status left the public status wrong. | Pause and resume set the season status unconditionally (idempotent), and every cycle reconciles the season status with the world. Tests cover crash scenarios with controls and a reconcile test. |
| 2 | Medium | A rejected advance would be replayed under the same key forever, wedging the world. | Keys include the command position, which changes with every recorded command. A rejection marks the world `failed` with the alert `runner_advance_rejected`. |
| 3 | Medium | Due ticks used the process clock (skew). | Production uses the database clock (`databaseNow`), the same clock that decides lease expiry. |
| 4 | Medium | Backlog "metrics" were only logs and columns; the probe missed failing cycles. | Prometheus `/metrics` endpoint; the probe fails on the last cycle's failure. |
| 5 | Medium | Any operator ID could send controls, and extensions were unbounded. | `RUNNER_CONTROL_OPERATORS` allowlist (`*` refused in production), a per-control extension cap and a database bound of 30 days. Tests cover both. Full RBAC is Phase 21. |
| 6 | Low | Failures zeroed the stored backlog, and `lastFailure` was not cleared when a cycle threw. | The backlog is kept on failure; the cycle bookkeeping is in a `finally`. |
| 7 | Low | A slow batch longer than the TTL loses the lease. | Kept by design (safe: standby, then re-acquire with a new token); documented in ADR 0008. |
| 8 | Low | The crash test was weaker than claimed. | It now counts boundaries and requires every one to fire, includes controls, and asserts season and decision equality. |
| 9 | Low | Step at the end of the season, step text after a crash, a stale approval, and a ceiling fixed at row creation. | Step at the end is rejected; the result text is fixed; approvals are cleared when reached; the ceiling follows configuration. |

## Compatibility, rollout and rollback

- **Migration 0006** only creates new tables. Existing worlds get a runner row on their first cycle (from their season).
- **`commitCommand`'s `fence`** is optional, so existing callers are unchanged.
- **Rollout.** Deploy the migration, then the simulation process with `RUNNER_CONTROL_OPERATORS` set. Run one replica, or more: the lease keeps them safe, with failover within the TTL.
- **Rollback.**
  - `0006 down` drops runner state and controls. It refuses once a schedule extension has been applied; roll forward instead.
  - Rolling the code back leaves worlds un-advanced; nothing is corrupted.
  - Setting `RUNNER_ENABLED=false` stops advancement without a deploy rollback.
