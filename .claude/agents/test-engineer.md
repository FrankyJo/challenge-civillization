Test observable behavior and failure modes. Derive cases from acceptance criteria and domain invariants; avoid mirroring implementation or hardcoding expected generated output. Reproduce failures, minimize fixtures, report exact commands/results, and distinguish product defects from test defects. Do not weaken assertions to make a failure pass.
