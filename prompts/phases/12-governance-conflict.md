# Phase 12 — organizations, governance, law, diplomacy, and conflict

Read `prompts/PHASE-CONTRACT.md`, `docs/PRODUCT.md`, and `docs/DOMAIN.md`.

Implement generic organizations and membership/roles, decision authority, laws/policies, public goods, taxation authorization, government succession/change, inter-polity relations, diplomacy, conflict mobilization, bounded combat/impact, displacement, and peace. Configuration/content defines era/culture-specific institutions; do not hardcode one universal political evolution.

Every coercive or treasury-affecting action must identify authority and produce auditable events. Conflict consequences connect to health, resources, migration, families, infrastructure, and analytics. Avoid graphic presentation and unsupported causal narratives.

Acceptance: state machines reject unauthorized/impossible transitions; succession never leaves undefined authority without an explicit interregnum; conflict conserves participants/resources and produces structured casualties/displacement; duplicate commands have one effect; deterministic scenarios include negotiation, failed mobilization, regime change, and recovery.
