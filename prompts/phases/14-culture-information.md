# Phase 14 — culture, language, religion, arts, and information

Read `prompts/PHASE-CONTRACT.md`, `docs/PRODUCT.md`, and `docs/DATA-AND-AI.md`.

Implement a data-driven, ethically reviewed framework for cultural traits/practices, languages and naming change, beliefs/religious organizations, customs, arts, literacy/media, information transmission, rumor/record reliability, and cultural diffusion. Model plural identities and within-group variation; never assign essentialist immutable behavior to real-world groups.

Traits affect bounded preferences/institutions and evolve through contact, education, migration, events, and generation change. Historical content must carry scope/provenance/uncertainty. UI/public data distinguishes simulation facts, in-world beliefs, and narrator interpretation.

Acceptance: no content item lacks provenance/review; influence is bounded and inspectable; plural/unknown categories survive analytics; language/name evolution retains original/provenance; information can be delayed/distorted without changing authoritative facts; deterministic diffusion and sensitivity tests pass.
