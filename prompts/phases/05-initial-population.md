Read `prompts/PHASE-CONTRACT.md`; use `/demographic-model`, `/historical-research`, and `/quality-gates` as needed.

Create versioned, licensed seed content for cultures, languages, naming conventions, era-valid names, and initial settlement/household distributions appropriate to 1 CE in the fictional world. Implement joint population synthesis for exactly 10,000 living people: age, recorded sex/gender fields, kinship, guardianship, household, culture, location, health baseline, capabilities, and name provenance.

Do not use subscriber identities or imply that a fictional culture is a caricature of a real group. Expose configuration/calibration distributions and validation reports. Names must use deterministic weighted choice and a documented fallback chain.

Acceptance: exact living count 10,000; no impossible parent ages/times, duplicate identity IDs, missing guardianship states, invalid household/location references, or out-of-era names; every name records dataset/version/fallback; same seed reproduces the population hash; distribution reports are plausible and reviewed; generation fits the benchmark budget.
